how to

we're going to roughly follow the timeline of the itinerary, though as the day goes on we may get a little off schedule! feel free to text me for confirmation. in between, i will be biking, and you are free -- encouraged, even -- to join me on a bike (classic or e-), board, blades, skates, scooter, or whatever else you can legally take on the route. join for a whole day of riding and shenanigans; join for a single leg and a couple shenanigans; or just meet up for a single shenanigan.

itinerary

all items take place on saturday, aug 1, 2026

  • 9 am: coffee and pastries at hard times cafe. reminder: cash only! make it rain on the punx. departing at 9:45 am

  • 1:45 pm: at river's request demand, we will be stringing up a piñata in minnehaha falls park. this is the endpoint of the birthday ride, after which we'll probably set river loose at a playground or something
