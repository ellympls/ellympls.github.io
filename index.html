how to

if you're reading this, it's because i'd like to spend my birthday with you. i want to do a few things on my birthday, and i'd like the people i care about to have the opportunity to join as much or as little as they like. if none of this is your speed, please hit me up so we can spend some time together.

the way it works is like this: we're going to roughly follow the timeline of the itinerary (though as the day goes on we may get a little off schedule! feel free to text me for confirmation), and in between, i will be biking, and you are free -- encouraged, even -- to join me on a bike (classic or e-), board, blades, skates, scooter, or whatever else you can legally take on the route. join for a whole day of riding and shenanigans; join for a single leg and a couple shenanigans; or just meet up for a single shenanigan.

itinerary

all items take place on saturday, aug 1, 2026

  • 9 am: coffee and pastries at hard times cafe. departing at 9:45 am
  • ride from htc to wac. about half an hour, just skirting the edges of downtown
  • 10:15 am: walker art center free first saturday featuring minneapolis puppetry weirdness! bring your inner child. departing around 11:30 am
  • the longest leg of the tour de birf. featuring several minneapolis' beautiful lakes and minnehaha creek!
  • 12:30 pm: pizza at northern fires pizza. depart at 1:30 pm
  • ride to minnehaha park
  • 1:45 pm: at river's request demand, we will be stringing up a piñata in minnehaha falls park. this is the endpoint of the birthday ride.
